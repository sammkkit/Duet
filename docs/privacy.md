# TwinShot Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

TwinShot is a camera app that takes photos and videos with your phone's front and back cameras. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **nothing leaves your phone.**

## What the app uses

| Permission | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Camera | To show the live preview and take photos and videos | Nowhere. Frames are processed on your phone only |
| Microphone | To record sound with your videos (asked only when you switch to Video) | Nowhere. Audio is stored inside the video file on your phone |
| Photos / storage (save only) | To save your photos and videos to your gallery (Android: DCIM/Camera, iOS: Photos) | Your own gallery. The app does not read your other photos |

If you deny the microphone, videos are recorded without sound. If you deny the camera, the app cannot be used.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect, store, or send any personal data to us or anyone else.
- It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
- It does not require an account or sign-in.
- It does not connect to the internet for any of its features.

Your photos and videos stay on your device until you choose to share them using another app.

## Children

TwinShot is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if crash reporting is added), this page will be updated and the date above changed before that version of the app is released.
